AIRIS

AI-CV to assist visually inspired people (Blind Assistant)
Can also help in personnel security forces
This AI-CV product is designed to assist visually impaired individuals and support personnel security forces.
It is a wireless device that works seamlessly with a mobile application, which handles all the processing and provides the user with accurate output.
We are mindful of the profound impact this could have and continue to refine our approach to best serve these communities.

Signify

AI-Powered Sign Language Interpreter
Bridging communication gaps for the hearing and speech impaired
This AI-driven system translates sign language into text and speech in real time, making communication seamless for individuals with hearing or speech impairments.
It comes as a wireless device paired with a mobile application, which processes sign gestures and provides instant output to the user.
We are committed to enhancing accessibility and inclusivity through continuous improvements in this technology.
